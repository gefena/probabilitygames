## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Probability Bingo card in Go Deeper tier after Remove One
The Go Deeper tier on the home page SHALL include a card for Probability Bingo, positioned after the Remove One card.

#### Scenario: Go Deeper tier shows Probability Bingo
- **WHEN** a user views the Go Deeper tier on the home page
- **THEN** a Probability Bingo card is visible after Remove One with title, emoji, and description
