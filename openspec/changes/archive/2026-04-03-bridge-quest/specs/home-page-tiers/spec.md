## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bridge Quest card in Go Deeper tier after Climber Race
The Go Deeper tier on the home page SHALL include a card for Bridge Quest, positioned after the Climber Race card.

#### Scenario: Go Deeper tier shows Bridge Quest
- **WHEN** a user views the Go Deeper tier on the home page
- **THEN** a Bridge Quest card is visible after Climber Race with title, emoji, and description
