## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Pizza Builder card in Go Deeper tier after Probability Bingo
The Go Deeper tier on the home page SHALL include a card for Pizza Builder, positioned after the Probability Bingo card.

#### Scenario: Go Deeper tier shows Pizza Builder
- **WHEN** a user views the Go Deeper tier on the home page
- **THEN** a Pizza Builder card is visible after Probability Bingo with title, emoji, and description
