## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Remove One card in Go Deeper tier after Roll & Race
The Go Deeper tier on the home page SHALL include a card for Remove One, positioned after the Roll & Race card.

#### Scenario: Go Deeper tier shows Remove One
- **WHEN** a user views the Go Deeper tier on the home page
- **THEN** a Remove One card is visible after Roll & Race with title, emoji, and description
